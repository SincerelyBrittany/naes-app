Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Nae's Place (“we,” “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect on this website for Finicky Felicia by Narene Russell, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Contact form: name, email, optional phone, subject, and message when you write to us.
  • Newsletter: email address if you subscribe (via Mailchimp or a similar provider).
  • Analytics: if Google Analytics is enabled, we may collect anonymized usage data such as pages viewed, approximate location, and device type.

How we use information

  • To respond to messages and book-related questions
  • To send newsletter updates you opted into (events, new books, tips)
  • To understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it

Third-party services

We may use EmailJS or Netlify Forms for contact messages, Mailchimp for email, Google Analytics for traffic insights, and retail partners (Amazon, Barnes & Noble) when you click “Buy.” Those sites have their own privacy policies. Purchases completed on Amazon or Barnes & Noble are handled by those companies—not by us.

Cookies

Analytics and newsletter tools may use cookies or similar technologies. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Children

This site promotes a children’s book and is intended for parents, caregivers, and educators. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from email using the link in any newsletter. To request deletion of contact-form data we hold, email us using the address on the contact section of the site.

Changes

We may update this policy occasionally. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Use the contact form on the homepage or reach out via Instagram @naes.place.
